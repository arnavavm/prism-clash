Rules & Card Guide

Objective: Empty your hand first! Match the top discard card by Colour, Number, or Symbol, or play a Wild card.

Colour-Blind Suit Emblems
🔥 Crimson — Flame
☀️ Gold — Sun
🍃 Emerald — Leaf
🌊 Cobalt — Wave
⊘ Skip (20 pts): Next player loses their turn.
⇄ Reverse (20 pts): Reverses play order (2-player: acts as Skip).
+2 Draw Two (20 pts): Next player draws 2 cards & skips.
✦ Wild (50 pts): Play anytime and choose the next suit.
✦+4 Wild Draw Four (50 pts): Choose colour + next player draws 4 & skips. Legal only if holding no matching colour card — otherwise opponents can challenge!

KING! Rule: When down to 1 card, tap KING! before the next turn begins, or opponents can catch you for +2 penalty cards!
