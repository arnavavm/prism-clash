Rules & Card Guide

Objective: Empty your hand first! Match the discard pile by Colour, Number, or Symbol, or play a Wild card.

Colour-Blind Emblems
🔥 Crimson — Flame
☀️ Gold — Sun
🍃 Emerald — Leaf
🌊 Cobalt — Wave
⊘ Skip (20 pts): Next player loses turn.
⇄ Reverse (20 pts): Reverse direction (2-player: acts as Skip).
+2 Draw Two (20 pts): Next player draws 2 cards & skips.
✦ Wild (50 pts): Play anytime, pick next colour.
✦+4 Wild Draw Four (50 pts): Pick colour + next player draws 4 & skips. Legal only if holding no current colour cards (otherwise challengeable!).

PRISM! Rule: When down to 1 card, press PRISM! before next turn begins. If caught, draw +2 penalty cards!
